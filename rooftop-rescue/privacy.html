Rooftop Rescue Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Rooftop Rescue: Don’t Fall! ("the game") is a mobile game published by AJ Arcade Labs. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information the game stores on your device

Your best scores, coins, unlocked runners and cities, missions and settings are saved only on your device. We do not run any server for the game, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

In-app purchases

You can buy coin packs and Remove Ads inside the game. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see your card, UPI or bank details. Google tells the game only which item was bought and a purchase receipt, which the game keeps on your device so a purchase is never lost or counted twice. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, app usage and ad interactions. Google uses this information as described in its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in a region where consent is required (for example the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android phones).

Rewarded ads

Some features, such as reviving the runner or doubling the coins from a run, are offered in exchange for watching an ad. Watching these ads is always optional.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all game data saved on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
