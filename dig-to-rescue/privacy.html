Dig to Rescue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how the Dig to Rescue mobile game ("the game") handles your information.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. It does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data only on your own device:

  • which levels you have completed and the stars and coins you earned,
  • your sound and music settings,
  • your graphics quality setting.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Internet and permissions

The game works fully offline and does not send anything over the internet. It does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
