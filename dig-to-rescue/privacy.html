Dig to Rescue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how the Dig to Rescue mobile game ("the game") handles your information.

Information we collect

We don't run any servers or accounts, and we don't collect or sell your personal information ourselves. The game does show ads, which are provided by Google (see Advertising below).

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data only on your own device:

  • which levels you have completed and the stars and coins you earned,
  • your sound and music settings,
  • your graphics quality setting.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels, and optional rewarded ads for extra hints. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, app usage and ad interactions. Google uses this information as described in its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Internet and permissions

The game can be played offline. It uses the internet only to load ads. It does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
